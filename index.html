IIMXamday

Your Term V exams, in your calendar

EMP MMS Batch 02, IIM Indore. Enter your roll number to see only the exams for courses you are enrolled in, then subscribe so dates stay current when the schedule changes.

Made with ❤ by Adarsh

On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen to install this app.

Exam dates are tentative, as published in the Term V schedule. Calendar events are 75 minutes, matching one session slot. Where the schedule gives no start time, the Session I slot (9:00 AM) is used and marked. All times are IST.
